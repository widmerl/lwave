Inspector

        
Click a key, dial, slider or switch to see what the firmware reads from it.

      

      

        
Playing

        

        

        
        
Computer keys play rows B–E: 2–9, Q–O, A–K, Z–M. Hold Shift to strike softly. Drag dials and sliders up or down (with Shift for fine steps); double-click resets. Click a switch to step it.

        

      

      

        
Connection

        
Plug the synth into USB, then connect. The page asks the firmware for its monitor stream (command m) and shows the panel exactly as the synth reads it: knob positions after calibration and smoothing, key travel, notes, switches and LEDs.
